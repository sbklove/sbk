PROPRIETARY HARDWARE ENGINEERING

Engineered for Zero Failure Demands

Our display cabinets are constructed using CNC-machined die-cast magnesium alloy, advanced driver ICs, and high-efficiency thermal architectures.

Fine Pixel Pitch & Ultra HD

Sub-millimeter MicroLED COB architecture down to P0.9. Crystal-clear 8K resolution at close viewing distances with 7,680 Hz refresh rate and zero flicker.

• MicroLED & Flip-Chip COB

Energy-Saving Common Cathode

Independent voltage distribution for Red (2.8V) and Green/Blue (3.8V) diodes cuts power consumption by up to 45% while keeping surface temperature under 38°C.

Zero rear access required. Vacuum extraction tool replaces modules in under 3 seconds. Ideal for flush architectural wall mounts and space-constrained lobbies.

• 3-Second Vacuum Extraction
